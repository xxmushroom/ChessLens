Welcome to ChessLens

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder.
2. Double-click ChessLens.exe.
3. Select board, capture it, review the pieces and confirm the position.

Python, PowerShell and a separate Stockfish installation are not required in a complete Windows build.
Keep ChessLens.exe, _internal and stockfish together. Do not move just the executable.
Set side to move, orientation, castling and en-passant in Settings before capture.
Use the Original capture tab to compare the detected pieces.
Open the interactive report for continuation playback and board flipping.
Stop / Escape stops tracking and practice clicks.
Reports and logs are saved under %LOCALAPPDATA%\ChessLens.
Use for local practice, post-game study, or games that permit engine assistance.
